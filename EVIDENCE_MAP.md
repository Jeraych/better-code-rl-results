# Experiments and results evidence map

This map links the retained experiment records, result fields and three repository plots. The individual final report and manuscript sources are separate and excluded. **Recomputed** means arithmetic over saved records, without rerunning generated programs, training or Judge requests. **Retained** means historical results/protocols are available, but their original analyzer or experiment was not rerun for this revision. Neither status establishes exact historical retraining; the new snapshot's check scope is recorded in [verification/](verification/).
